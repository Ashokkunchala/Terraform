# Day 60 — Capstone 3 and Interview Bootcamp

Design a multi-account, multi-region platform with provider aliases, isolated state, centralized CI, least-privilege roles, policy gates and disaster recovery. Incident drills: state-lock failure, drift, failed deployment, IAM denial and regional failure. Final evidence: architecture, working Terraform, automated validation, security/policy results, cost analysis, recovery test and interview notes. Graduation: explain an unfamiliar repo, its graph/state, risks, safe change and operational consequences.
